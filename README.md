# ppppso Umbrel app store

A community app store for [umbrelOS](https://umbrel.com) 2.0.

| App | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Backup for MEGA](https://github.com/ppppso/mega-backup) | Two-way sync of a MEGA folder plus nightly restic snapshots |

## Add it to your Umbrel

1. Open the App Store, then the **⋯** menu at the top right, then **Community App Stores**.
2. Paste `https://github.com/ppppso/umbrel-app-store` and click **Add**.
3. Open the **ppppso** store and install the app.
